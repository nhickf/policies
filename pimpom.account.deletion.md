
## Page 1: Delete your Pimpom account

Pimpom is made by 16bits.dev. You can delete your Pimpom account and its data at any time, with or without the app installed.

### Delete it in the app

1. Open Pimpom and tap the lock icon on Home to open **Privacy**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

### Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email **\[deletion email\]** from any address with the subject "Delete my Pimpom account". Tell us the name shown on your Pimpom profile and whether you signed in with Google or Apple, so we can find your account. We confirm by email and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

### Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or the App Store, so cancel there first: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

### What gets deleted

| Data | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Your account and profile name | Deleted |
| Your connections, for both people in each | Deleted |
| Pings sent and received in those connections | Deleted |
| Invite codes you created | Deleted |
| Devices registered for notifications | Deleted |
| Your plan and pass records on our server | Deleted |
| Pings, nicknames and settings stored on your phone | Removed when you uninstall the app |

### What we keep

Records of purchases stay with Google Play or the App Store and with RevenueCat, our purchase processor. They are kept as long as those companies are required to keep them for tax, accounting and refunds. They contain your purchase history, not your connections or pings.
