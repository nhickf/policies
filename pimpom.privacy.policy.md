# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 October 2026**

Pimpom is made by 16bits.dev ("we"). It lets two people send each other small, private pings. We collect only what the app needs to work, we don't show ads, and we don't sell your data.

### What we collect

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| An ID from Google or Apple when you sign in | To recognise your account. We don't store your Google or Apple password. |
| Your display name | Shown to the people you pair with. It starts as the name on your Google or Apple account (or your email address if there's no name), and you can change it in the app. |
| Your connections and pairing requests | To know who you're paired with and to handle invite codes and approvals. |
| Pings: the symbol, its label, who sent it and when | To deliver pings to the person you're paired with. |
| Your vibration pattern | Played on your partner's phone when your ping arrives. |
| A notification token and whether your phone is Android or iPhone | To send you notifications. |
| Your plan or pass and when it ends | To unlock paid features. Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card details. |
| The times you try to pair with a code | To block repeated guessing of invite codes. |

**Kept only on your phone:** your ping designs and history, nicknames you give people, your app lock PIN (stored scrambled, never in plain text), and your privacy and appearance settings. We can't see these.

**We don't collect:** your location, contacts, photos, or advertising IDs. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

### Who we share it with

We share data only with services that run the app for us:

- **Supabase**: hosts our database and server.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** and **Apple Push Notification service**: deliver notifications.
- **Google Sign-In** and **Sign in with Apple**: let you sign in.
- **RevenueCat**, **Google Play** and the **App Store**: process purchases and tell us your plan.

The people you pair with see your display name, your vibration pattern, and the pings you send them. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

### How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete everything above from our server straight away. Purchase records stay with Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat as long as they're required to keep them.

### Your choices and rights

- **See or correct your data:** your name and connections are in the app; email us for a copy of the rest.
- **Delete your account:** in the app under Privacy → Delete account, or see \[deletion page URL\].
- **Turn off notifications:** in your phone's settings.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in the Philippines, that's the National Privacy Commission.

### Security

Data travels between the app and our server encrypted (HTTPS), and our database can't be read directly from the app; only our server and our team can access it.

### Age

Pimpom is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.

### Data stored abroad

Our service providers may store data on servers outside your country. We use providers that protect it as described here.

### Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
